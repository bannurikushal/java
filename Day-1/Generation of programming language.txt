                              Generation of programming!
First Generation of program:
                                =>1st genereration of computers are known as machine language. 
                                =>Which understand only binary numbers(0's&1's).
                                =>No need any translate.
Advantages:
           =>Easy to communicate to computer.
           =>It takes less time to communicate.
Disadvantages:
            =>Difficult to understand the binary language.
            =>Difficult to debug the program.

Second generation of program:
                              =>2nd generation of computers also low level language.
                              =>The program consists of "Mnemonics".
                              =>it as translator known as "Assembler".

Disadvantages:

             =>It takes more time to communicate to computer.

Third generation of program:
                             =>3rd generation of computer some time known as Low level language & High level language.
                             =>C program is 3rd generation program.
                             =>It as compiler to compile the program.

          =>Easy to write the program.
          =>The program contains the some basic English words.
